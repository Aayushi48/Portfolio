Case Study 01
1Cell.Ai Workflow Management

Workflow Management & Contextual Collaboration

Designing a centralized, sample-specific communication experience for an integrated multi-omics platform connected with LIMS.

My Role: Requirement Gathering • Feature Prioritization • Functional Validation • UAT Leadership

The Challenge

  • Multi-stakeholder sample lifecycle (onboarding, wet lab, NGS, analysis, review, and report release) relied on fragmented spreadsheets and chat apps.
  • Queries were disconnected from the respective sample record, leaving no centralized audit history or traceability.
  • Earlier-stage stakeholders lacked visibility into downstream review blockers, complicating sample revisits.

Product Solution

  • Built a centralized contextual chat window natively bound to each sample workflow upon onboarding.
  • Lifecycle-aware visibility tracking sample status progression with role-based permissions.
  • In-thread query management (@mentions, status flags, resolved toggles) and evidence sharing (IGV visualization screenshots).

Outcomes & Value

  • Single source of truth for all sample-related clinical discussions, eliminating multi-tool switching.
  • Complete audit trail retained across the sample lifecycle for regulatory compliance and review speed.
  • Streamlined query resolution and faster turnaround time between wet-lab and reporting teams.

Case Study 02
1Cell.Ai Genomics & Visualization

Fusion Analysis Optimization & Visualization

Making gene fusion analysis more accurate, biologically interpretable, and time-efficient within an oncology genomics platform.

My Role: Secondary Research • Parameter Evaluation • Threshold Refinement • UI/UX Visualization Design • Cross-Functional Coordination

The Challenge

  • The legacy fusion detection tool produced an overwhelming volume of false-positive candidate fusions, burdening analysts with tedious manual filtering.
  • Critical biological parameters (exon junctions, reading frame status, orientation) were missing from the interface.
  • Analysts had to manually cross-reference external databases to assess the pathogenic significance of fusion events.

Product Solution

  • Re-evaluated detection algorithms alongside the CTO and bioinformatics team to tighten parameter thresholds and suppress noise.
  • Redesigned the analyst UI into structured, high-density parameter tiles displaying essential metrics at a glance.
  • Introduced unidirectional and bidirectional orientation indicators, alongside interactive exon-level and functional protein-domain diagrams.

Outcomes & Value

  • Drastically decreased false-positive fusion findings, saving significant review time per oncology case.
  • Transformed dense bioinformatic outputs into clear, visual domain-level and exon-level representations.
  • Accelerated reporting turnaround time (TAT) while enhancing clinical confidence for actionable fusions (e.g., ALK, ROS1, RET).
Oncology Genomics Gene Fusion Detection Data Visualization Threshold Calibration Bioinformatics UI CTO Collaboration
View on Notion
Technical & Scientific Approach

Benchmarked existing literature on fusion caller specificity and identified recurring false-positive motifs. Formulated parameter cutoff recommendations (split read counts, spanning pairs, breakpoint homology) and collaborated directly with bioinformatics engineers to implement the pipeline updates.

Information Architecture & UI Tiles

Organized candidate fusions into modular visual tiles showing: 5′ and 3′ gene partners, exact chromosomal breakpoints, reading frame retention (in-frame vs out-of-frame), preserved tyrosine kinase or catalytic domains, and reciprocal orientation.

Product Management Takeaway

Demonstrated the ability to bridge deep computational biology with user-centered interface design — balancing analytical rigor with frictionless usability to directly accelerate clinical turnaround time.

Case Study 03
Strand Life Sciences Healthcare AI & NLP

Biomedical Literature Intelligence Tool

Building a natural language processing (NLP) intelligence tool to extract and connect biomedical entities across oncology and chronic diseases.

My Role: Scientific Data Annotation • NLP Model Validation • Human-in-the-Loop Quality Assurance • Product Usability Inputs

The Challenge

  • Biomedical literature holds massive unstructured knowledge (oncology, women's health, diabetes, obesity) that is prohibitively time-consuming to mine manually.
  • Early-stage NLP models struggled to reliably identify and link complex medical entities (mutations, genes, drugs, phenotypes).
  • Lack of high-quality, standardized clinical training corpora hindered model precision and recall.

Product Solution

  • Executed a 3-phase NLP product development framework: Paragraph-level annotation ➔ Model output verification ➔ UI usability design.
  • Utilized the BRAT annotation environment to systematically tag entities (biomarkers, drugs, diseases, symptoms) and relationship triplets.
  • Established a human-in-the-loop review pipeline to detect model hallucinations, missing relations, and classification discrepancies.

Outcomes & Value

  • Successfully shipped a functional Beta literature intelligence tool empowering researchers to explore entity-relationship graphs.
  • Substantially improved NLP model training accuracy through rigorous, domain-curated scientific annotation sets.
  • Compressed literature synthesis times for complex clinical variant investigations from hours to minutes.

Case Study 04
1Cell.Ai Variant Automation

Automating Clinical Variant Classification & Interpretation

Streamlining clinical genomics interpretation by integrating evidence from ClinGen, VarSome, and GeneBe into an automated classification pipeline.

My Role: Product Feature Coordination • Clinical Workflow Optimization • Evidence-based Classification • ACMG/AMP Rule Mapping

The Challenge

  • Variant interpretation is the primary bottleneck in clinical genomics; analysts must evaluate dozens of external knowledge portals per sample.
  • Repetitive manual lookup across external sites led to cognitive fatigue and lengthened reporting turnaround time (TAT).
  • Risk of inter-analyst discordance when classifying Pathogenic (P) and Likely Pathogenic (LP) variants.

Product Solution

  • Conceptualized and coordinated an automated variant classification pipeline adhering to ACMG/AMP clinical standards.
  • Integrated live evidence streaming from established resources: ClinGen, VarSome, and GeneBe.
  • Surfaced pre-computed evidence codes, population allele frequencies, and predictive in-silico metrics directly inside the analyst's workspace.

Outcomes & Value

  • Dramatically curtailed repetitive manual database searches for pathogenic and likely pathogenic variants.
  • Standardized classification criteria across the entire scientific curation team for consistent report quality.
  • Significantly shortened interpretation turnaround time while preserving full clinical accountability and oversight.
